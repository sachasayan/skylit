# Clock visual review — round 1

**Verdict: REVISE.** Reviewed the approved target and all four six-shape sheets, plus original medium/tall/strip and the suspect wide/minimum crops. The shared cream, olive, terracotta palette and serif numerals are a promising foundation, but visible artwork clipping and shallow-layout composition remain blockers. This is a visual review of the synthetic 10:24 renders, not proof of other time strings, themes, motion or device behavior.

## Priority blockers

1. **Landscape: the sun is chopped flat across its top.** In minimum/wide/medium/full, and to a lesser extent strip, the landscape viewport clips the disk at its upper boundary. The approved concept shows a circular sun rising behind hills, not a flat-topped polygon. Move the disk down/resize it or enlarge the art viewport while preserving the text exclusion region. The back hill also begins with a long horizontal shelf at that boundary; use a curve with a natural entry slope.
2. **Flip: decoration collides with the useful composition.** In wide, the shelf runs through the date's upper area and the leaves appear as fragments immediately behind the tile edge; medium/full likewise hide almost the whole stem behind the minute tile. Reserve a clear lower-right plant region, shrink the tiles/plant if needed, or omit the plant. Date, period and complete tile silhouettes must read cleanly. The target plant is a small, distinct object below/beside the tiles.
3. **Strip responsiveness fails the glanceability goal.** Analog puts a roughly 90 px face and tiny date in a centered vertical stack inside a 1008×142 module; almost the entire width is unused. Botanical puts a modest 10:24 at the far left, a tiny disconnected plant at far right, and an enormous wash between. Flip packs its tiles/date into the first quarter of the strip. Use horizontal compositions and scale the primary time to available height: larger Analog face with date beside it; balanced, centered Flip tile/date group; larger Botanical time with adjacent date/period and omit its tiny strip plant.

## Per-style findings

### Landscape (`classic`)

- **Minimum:** legible live hierarchy; sun clipping and flat hill entry are defects.
- **Wide:** clear centered hierarchy, but time is cautious relative to width and hill height. Increasing time modestly would better match the target's dominant numeral scale. Sun clipping is a blocker.
- **Medium/full:** strongest base composition. Correct sun clipping; soften the abrupt art boundary/hill entry. The current artwork is substantially flatter and more geometric than the target's quiet rolling hills.
- **Tall:** text is anchored at top and scenery at bottom, leaving a very large empty middle. This is an aesthetic/composition weakness rather than clipping. Move the whole time/date group closer to the vertical center of the upper clear region, or bring the hills up somewhat. The width-constrained time itself is sensibly sized.
- **Strip:** separate text/art zones are coherent. The AM is stranded near the bottom while date is nearly against the upper edge; tighten the time/date/period group and retain comfortable padding. The disk slightly clips at the card top.

### Analog (`analog`)

- **Minimum:** readable restrained dial/date, no blocker beyond optional face polish.
- **Wide:** face/date side-by-side is the correct responsive direction. Plant crowds the date's left/bottom edge and dial; remove it at this height or place it well clear of date. Composition is otherwise coherent.
- **Medium/full:** visually the closest successful translation. Keep the airy dial and fine ticks. Plant leaf silhouettes are rougher/more repetitive than the target, but acceptable local-vector adaptation once scaled and separated cleanly.
- **Tall:** clean, readable centered face/date with no clipping. Large blank bands are a consequence of preserving the dial circle, not an automatic defect. Do not fill them gratuitously.
- **Strip:** blocker above; enlarge the dial and move date alongside. Current date-under-face makes an unnecessarily small clock.
- The target's finer rim and slightly longer minute hand would improve delicacy; these are refinements, not blockers. The hands correctly show 10:24; no second hand is required.

### Flip (`stacked`)

- **Minimum:** attractive, readable tiles with a clear split seam. Good omission of decoration.
- **Wide:** plant/shelf/date collision is the clearest defect. Resolve before polishing material.
- **Medium/full:** tile numerals are excellent at a glance. Recompose lower-right plant so the leaf spray is not just a fringe protruding from behind the tile; give shelf a clear relationship to plant and date.
- **Tall:** horizontal tiles retained appropriately; date/period centered and no clipping. Empty vertical space is acceptable, although stronger warm surface depth could make this feel more intentional.
- **Strip:** 1008 px card containing a tiny left-aligned group feels like an unfinished responsive mode. Center/rebalance the group and use adjacent date/period; keep a genuine date/time gap.
- Tile rounding/shadows are good. A subtle upper/lower value difference could improve flip-card materiality; the nearly uniform olive currently feels a little flat. This is refinement after composition.

### Botanical (`digital`)

- **Minimum:** time is strong; tiny plant looks like a stray sprig. Omit it, consistent with the small-module design instruction.
- **Wide:** generous time/date/period, but plant is crowded into the right corner. Keep plant subordinate and leave clear breathing room around the numeral 4.
- **Medium/full:** the plant is too large relative to the target's small supporting branch; its large leaves compete directly with the last numeral. Reduce plant width/height and preserve a distinct text-clear area. The wash is warm but reads as a single oversized hard-edged diagonal oval; the target is a quieter irregular translucent wash behind the typography. Ease edge/opacity and make its scale composition-specific.
- **Tall:** the oversized branch fills almost the entire bottom half. This is a concept-fidelity/composition issue, not unintended card-edge clipping. Reduce it and balance the typography against the quieter wash.
- **Strip:** blocker above; time lacks prominence and the small remote plant is unnecessary.

## Acceptance bar for the next pass

All four concepts must remain distinct. Fix actual sun clipping, Flip date/art collisions and strip scale/composition first. Then reduce Botanical decoration to the supporting role shown by the target. Keep enough material warmth through restrained gradients, curves and shadows; literal photorealistic texture is not required. Tall blank space alone, target/date differences and the absence of an analog seconds hand are not reasons to reject. Re-review the original crops, not only scaled contact sheets, after these changes.
