## Consequential discrepancies

1. **Wide/strip have an exposed vertical terrain cut.** In wide, all land begins at x≈229 of 496 with a vertical edge from y≈132 to the bottom. In strip, it begins at x≈464 of 1008, with a vertical edge from y≈95 to the bottom. The target scene is clipped only at the outer panel. A right-side responsive scene can begin at the bottom and climb smoothly, or continue a low foreground across the base; it should not reveal the rectangular extent of the illustration internally.
2. **Tall land dominates and becomes steep.** Tall left hill peaks around y≈358 and falls to the center around y≈500; the right hill rises sharply again. Green occupies a large lower-left mass. Time cap height is about 52 pixels while land height is about 258, around five times cap height, versus target land height roughly 150 versus time 110. The sun remains circular and about numeral height, but is nestled between steep slopes. This no longer reads as the low restrained rolling landscape beneath a dominant clock. The label ends near y≈251 and first hill starts around y≈358, leaving a large clear gap before a visually oversized scene.
3. **Serif glyph character is heavier and more ornamental.** Target `2` has a slender, plainly ending upper-left curve. Current `2` has a conspicuous rounded ball terminal, visible even at medium/wide. Target baseline serifs on `1` and `4` are thin horizontal feet; current feet are thick, blunt slabs with heavier lower junctions. Current `0` and `4` have the correct overall oval/closed-counter construction, but matching the family category alone is insufficient. Preserve stroke contrast while matching the lighter terminal/foot shapes. Exact font name remains unproven and is not required.
4. **Material is smooth and under-warm.** Current sky has a large pale/near-white upper-left area transitioning into peach toward the right. Target is more consistently peach across the numeral area and shows quiet mottling. Current hills are nearly flat solid fills, and sun has smooth tonal change; target surfaces have fine grain and pigment-like variation. Do not compensate with aggressive uniform digital noise, outlines, or physical hill thickness shadows.
5. **Near-square stack sits high and leaves an oversized AM-to-land gap.** Current date near y=.125–.15, time .22–.42, AM .49–.52, highest land .64. Target date .16–.19, time .28–.51, AM .58–.62, highest land .68. Thus current AM-to-highest-land gap is roughly .12–.13 height, about twice the target's .06. Date/time/AM remain centered, but this comparable shape should rebalance the stack and landscape spacing. The time spans about .80 width versus target .73; its visible cap height is about .20 card height versus target .24, giving a broader horizontal mass.
6. **Type value and minor hierarchy.** Current olive is visibly lighter/greener than target's dark olive. It stays legible but softens the time's figure/ground anchor. Minimum inline AM cap height is about .35–.4 of numeral height; target is nearer .17–.2. The inline arrangement is fine for compact use, but AM has more weight than necessary. Full AM is about one tenth of numeral height, noticeably smaller than target; date remains appropriately secondary.
