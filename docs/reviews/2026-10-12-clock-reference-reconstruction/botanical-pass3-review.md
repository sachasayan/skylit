## Pixel-grounded interpretation checks

The approved target supports a sturdy serif time and varied broad five-leaf sprig; both are visible in these captures. It does **not** require one exact serif family, species, paint medium, lobe count or clock/branch coordinate. Those are not grounds for failure.

The clean separation requirement is independently visible in the concept: the upper branch blade lies to the right of the last digit with cream between them, and lower blades remain below the numeral line. Medium/full captures instead put leaf material underneath the `4` footprint. Allowing responsive recomposition does not erase this figure-ground distinction.

The wash comparison is more uncertain and less consequential: the reference shows a broader organic oblique silhouette, while the captures show a regular skinny ellipse. The inventory's uncertainty about continuous versus overlapping construction remains valid. This is a modest refinement issue, not evidence that wash must be implemented as two separate blobs.
