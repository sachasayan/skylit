## Consequential changes needed for fidelity

1. **Replace the delicate numeral character with sturdy, broad serif forms.** Test zero side-to-top stroke contrast, `1` flag/foot, `2` baseline, and `4` diagonal/crossbar/foot against the approved target. Keeping the same rust color and generic serif classification is insufficient.
2. **Rebuild the sprig silhouette before refining vein detail.** Restore meaningful variation in blade length, width, asymmetry, tip turns and direction; a broad left leaf, down/left lower leaf, and tall curved terminal leaf should emerge naturally from a curved attached branch. Do not merely rotate or enlarge the existing repeated ladder. Exact species or five-leaf count is not compulsory; the observed directional and proportional variety is.
3. **Make the wash subordinate and regain cream negative space in the roomy card.** Reduce its giant clipped swath character, preserve an organic oblique mass, and restore a subtle lower/right warm area where space permits. A single perfect oval or precise lobe-count recipe is not demanded.
4. **Restore AM's place in the hierarchy, especially full.** In the target it is visibly larger than date and around one-quarter to one-third of numeral height. Responsive layouts may vary that ratio, but full currently makes it a tiny date-like footnote despite ample space.
5. **Soften roomy panel profile and surface.** Broader tonal variation and quiet texture should carry the material; reduced speckle prominence and gentler comparable-square corners would better match the target.

## Responsive gate, independently assessed

- **Minimum:** Time/date/AM legible; reduced date and adjacent AM are sensible at 240×142. Branch omission is treated as a constrained simplification, not a sole failure. Its current numeral character remains a fidelity issue.
- **Wide:** Clear hierarchy, no collisions, complete branch, readable date/AM. There is abundant unused room while the sprig remains quite small, but that is primarily a composition/fidelity concern rather than overflow.
- **Medium:** Legible, no collision or visible clipping. Date/time/AM relationships are usable, although AM and branch are visually subordinate beyond the target's intended strength.
- **Tall:** Time/date/AM are clear at the top. The branch's right-facing leaves are visibly cut by the right boundary, leaving incomplete blade contours; its long quiet middle is occupied mainly by the giant wash. Reposition/recompose the branch rather than allowing accidental cuts. The target lets stems approach the edge while keeping recognizable principal leaf silhouettes.
- **Strip:** Time remains primary and date/AM are readable beside it. Ornament omission and lateral rearrangement are reasonable. No responsive failure from abandoning the square's exact coordinates.
- **Full:** All text readable, but AM is excessively small relative to both time and date despite ample space. This is the clearest hierarchy scaling failure. Branch is complete and clears text.
- **Near-square:** All content legible and separated. This is the closest composition for evaluating target identity and exposes the delicate glyphs, narrow repetitive sprig, oversized wash and tight corners most clearly.

Responsive pass requires readable and appropriately ranked time/date/AM in all supported shapes, complete intentional foliage when retained, and clean separation. It does not require square coordinates, a fixed branch gap, or every wash detail in narrow/strip variants. Fidelity pass additionally requires the specific sturdy type, varied curved sprig, quiet organic field, soft cream material and corresponding visual balance. Baseline meets neither full gate yet.
