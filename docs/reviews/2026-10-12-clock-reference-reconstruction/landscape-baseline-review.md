# Landscape baseline — direct target review

Reference: approved sheet, **top-middle Landscape**, evaluated using the locked inventory and its independent validation. Captures reviewed individually: classic-minimum.png, classic-wide.png, classic-medium.png, classic-tall.png, classic-strip.png, classic-full.png, classic-near-square.png, plus classic-sizes.png, all in before/. No source or earlier implementation verdict was inspected. This review gives no improvement credit.

## Gates

**Fidelity gate: FAIL.** The time is recognizably the intended contrast-serif clock, but the warm hill ordering and green foreground coverage contradict the approved scene. The near-square view also has a smaller sun relative to the numerals, a cream/yellow rather than peach sky, much tighter panel corners, and a typographic stack positioned high enough to leave a substantially larger AM-to-land gap. The object set alone does not establish fidelity.

**Responsive gate: FAIL for visual consistency; functional geometry/legibility largely passes.** All seven variants retain readable time, avoid type/land collisions, and show circular rather than stretched suns. Wide/strip left type with right-side landscape is a permissible recomposition. However, the sun is about half numeral height in minimum and more than twice numeral height in tall, with its right arc clipped in tall; this changes its visual role substantially. Tall has a large empty middle region and a giant scene below it. Other variants do not exhibit an obvious collision or clipping failure.

## Consequential deltas, in priority order

1. **Warm hill overlap is reversed.** Target: a terracotta left hill descends across the card in front of a lighter warm hill rising on the right. Baseline: the left slope is pale warm orange; a darker orange right slope rises in front, leaving the pale left material as a narrow ribbon above green. This changes the scene’s topology and color balance in every capture. Preserve a distinct light right hill behind the terracotta sweep; do not merely recolor the combined U-shaped horizon.
2. **Green covers the entire bottom edge.** Target green falls to the bottom at roughly .72 width, exposing terracotta on the lower-right remainder. Baseline green continues all the way right, sometimes with a small upturn/mound near the corner. The result is a green footer-like band. This relationship persists even in wide and strip; secondary simplification need not retain a full-width green blanket.
3. **Sun scale loses consistency.** Estimates from visible digit heights and complete disk geometry: minimum ≈.5× numeral height; wide ≈1.7–1.8×; medium ≈.85–.9×; tall ≈2.2–2.3× with right-edge crop; strip ≈1.25–1.3×; full ≈.75–.8×; near-square ≈.7–.75×. Target is ≈.9–.95×. Exact ratios are approximate, but the minimum/tall extremes and near-square undersizing are clear. No observed anisotropic sun stretching.
4. **The panel reads flatter and sharper-cornered.** Near-square corners are approximately .02–.03 panel width, versus the target’s broad rounded corners around .08–.09. Larger captures keep especially tight corners. The visible edge reads as a fine gray/tan outline rather than the reference’s warm raised rim. External shadow cannot be fully assessed because individual images end at the panel perimeter; do not infer unseen shadow geometry. The visible construction still lacks the target’s softly rounded panel impression.
5. **Sky/material are too neutral and granular.** Baseline has an ivory/yellow sky with dense, comparatively uniform fine noise. Target has a stronger peach warmth and softer low-contrast mottled/pigment variation. Grain exists, so this is weakened rather than missing texture. Lower right warm slope and green foreground have broad dark shading that reads more like a shaded digital surface than the flatter pigment-like target. No strong literal hill bevel is present, which is correct.
6. **Near-square hierarchy is shifted upward.** Baseline date is near .08–.10 height, time around .20–.45, AM around .54–.57; target date around .17–.19, time .28–.51, AM .58–.62. Positions can adapt, but in the most comparable shape this puts the date conspicuously close to the top and leaves ~.13 height of sky between AM and the highest scene, versus roughly .06 in the target. Full similarly leaves a large AM-to-land gap and makes AM notably small relative to numeral height.

## Per-size responsive observations

- **Minimum, 240×142:** Legible time/date; inline AM is an acceptable compact adaptation, although its cap height is nearly half numeral height and it competes more than target AM. Sun is a tiny bump behind the right slope. Corners are relatively more generous here. Land topology and full-width green remain wrong. Responsive legibility passes; visual identity is weakened.
- **Wide, 496×194:** Left-aligned date/time/AM and right landscape avoid collisions. Sun is oversized relative to digits and highest land has a comparatively steep shoulder where it begins near the lower middle. Recomposition itself is acceptable; exact square positions are not required.
- **Medium, 496×300:** Good type separation; circular sun prominence is closest to the target among these captures. Broad sky gap between AM and terrain and wrong overlap/bottom coverage remain. No functional responsive blocker seen.
- **Tall, 240×616:** Time/date/AM fit without clipping. The empty middle is large, while the sun is more than twice numeral height and clipped at the right. Land has a deep valley and steep exposed edge rather than low restrained hills. This is the clearest responsive visual failure.
- **Strip, 1008×142:** Type is readable; inline AM and shortened date are acceptable. Right-side scene does not enter the type. Sun is moderately larger than numeral height, but remains round; major topology remains wrong. No collision or overflow failure seen.
- **Full, 1008×616:** Serif glyphs remain clear and dominant. AM is only around one tenth of numeral cap height rather than around one fifth; sky between AM and hills is over-expanded. Sun is moderately undersized. Green fills the bottom edge and wrong warm-layer ordering remain clear.
- **Near-square, 496×542:** Best direct comparison to the square target. Glyph family is credible; scene placement and type gaps differ as described above. Circle is smaller relative to time, right warm hill is darker/in front, pale warm strip descends beneath it, and green blankets the entire base. Broad corner/peach material character is substantially weakened.

## What is already faithful

The time uses the correct serif construction rather than generic monoline digits; colon is round; secondary lettering is tracked uppercase; no extra scene objects were invented; the sun is a textured golden disk behind the terrain; the surfaces remain matte; and the land/type boundary is clear. These observations describe the current baseline only and do not grant credit for any prior iteration.
