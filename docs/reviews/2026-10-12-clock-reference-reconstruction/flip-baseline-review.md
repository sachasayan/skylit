# Flip baseline visual review

Scope: approved **top-right Flip** concept, locked inventory and its independent validation, then direct inspection of `stacked-sizes.png` and the seven individual `stacked-{minimum,wide,medium,tall,strip,full,near-square}.png` captures in `before/`. No source or earlier implementation verdicts inspected. This is an absolute comparison against the target; no improvement credit is assigned.

**Fidelity gate: FAIL. Responsive composition gate: PASS on the supplied captures, with the legibility limits below.** The implementation is recognizably a Flip clock and preserves core reading behavior. Its large-scale objects and decoration remain materially unlike the target: the corner plant/vase/shelf are compressed generic substitutes; the panel and tiles use conspicuous uniform fine noise; the material edge/hinge construction is weakened. These are independent of the allowed responsive reflow.

## Prioritized consequential deltas

1. **Restore the unconstrained corner arrangement as a constructed object.** Enlarge/recompose the plant's vertical reach and leftward fan; use curved continuous branches, varied narrow/broad turned leaves, and directional central veins. Give the vase a rounded pear belly/shoulders with compact base, soft asymmetric shade and sparse ceramic speckling. Give the shelf a readable receding top/front face and wood grain. These components are currently recognizable icons, but their defining silhouettes/material/contact relationships are weakened or contradicted. Constrained shapes may omit them.
2. **Replace generalized fine grain with distinct surface scales and shallow plate construction.** The panel needs broad quiet cream variation; tiles need gentle olive variation, a fine pale perimeter, local dark inner edge and soft short shadow. Preserve the current restrained depth while making the face/rim boundary visible. Treat hinges as olive components with small highlights, not tan bars. Preserve aligned numerals and keep seam thinner than strokes, adding the visible dark-upper/light-lower edge pairing.
3. **Rebalance ordinary near-square/full composition and secondary text.** Center the pair optically; retain useful breathing room. Keep functional date distinctly smaller than AM/time so it does not become a competing display row. Exact reference coordinates are not required for the different panel ratios. The near-square capture is the strongest location to judge this correction.

- **Tall (240×616): PASS.** Stacking is intentional and permitted. Reading order remains hours then minutes; AM/date fit below with no clipping. No penalty for omitted decoration or vertical geometry.
- **Strip (1008×142): PASS.** Two compact tiles plus date alongside and AM beneath fit cleanly. This is an allowed recomposition. Tiles are comparatively small; direct capture still reads `10 24`, AM and date. Final on-frame legibility requires native-size viewing, not zoomed montage inspection alone.
- **Full (1008×616): PASS.** Complete content fits with large time and no collision. Large date/underweight corner is a fidelity/hierarchy issue, not overflow.
- **Near-square (496×510): PASS.** Complete content fits. Left-shifted tile group and small corner arrangement are fidelity issues, not layout breakage.
