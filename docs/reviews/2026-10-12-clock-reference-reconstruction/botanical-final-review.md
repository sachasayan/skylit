## Bounded remaining deviations

1. Major wash is more elliptical/narrow/regular than the target’s fuller organic silhouette. It retains low contrast, diagonal organization and cream breathing room in the comparable card.
2. Larger foliage has stronger modeled shading and more articulated veins than the approved reference. The natural broad varied silhouettes remain the governing feature.
3. `2` has a rounder upper terminal. Overall broad sturdy serif character is intact.
4. Wide/full use a smaller branch relative to the time than the square concept; tall/strip reshape the wash substantially. These are visible recompositions within the approved responsive scope.
5. Dark-mode thin stems and subsidiary veins are quieter than in light mode, but principal leaf silhouettes, turns and attachments remain legible.

These are bounded visible differences, not consequential blockers in the supplied evidence. The result retains Botanical identity and readable, balanced presentation; it is not a claim of pixel-identical reconstruction.
