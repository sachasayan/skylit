## Defining observations and concrete comparison tests

### Typography and information hierarchy

- **Observed:** The time is the dominant object, rust/terracotta brown, on one line. Its bounding box is approximately x=.12–.80, y=.39–.60. The numerals occupy about two-thirds of the panel width and one-fifth of its height. There is substantial quiet space above them.
- **Observed:** The digits have a robust serif character and clear stroke contrast, rather than uniform geometric strokes. The `1` has a sloped upper flag and a substantial baseline foot; the `0` is a tall oval with heavier sides; the `2` has a curved upper bowl, descending diagonal, and firm horizontal foot; the `4` has an open triangular counter, right upright, and visible foot. Those recognizable glyph features are stronger identity tests than naming a particular font.
- **Observed:** The colon consists of two round, generously sized marks. Its dots are visibly separated, and its color and apparent weight belong to the numerals. It is not a small punctuation afterthought.
- **Observed:** The date uses small, thin, widely tracked uppercase olive letters, in two lines: `TUESDAY` then `APR 16`. It is left aligned at about x=.145. The date begins around y=.19 and ends around y=.29. Both lines have similar letter height, roughly one-sixth of numeral height. The day is the longer line.
- **Observed:** `AM` is a separate left-aligned olive label below the time, approximately x=.145, y=.65–.72. It is visibly larger than the date letters, roughly one-quarter to one-third of numeral height, but remains secondary. It does not sit beside the last digit or in the date block.
- **Test:** At normal viewing size, reading order should be time first, then date/AM, then botanical detail. A tall narrow sans numeral, a delicate high-contrast hairline serif, or small date-like `AM` would each lose distinct observed traits even if the bounding boxes matched.
- **Inference / uncertainty:** The exact typeface and whether there is subtle texture in its fill cannot be determined from this sheet. The evidence supports relatively sturdy serif numerals, not a mandatory font family. Date and AM appear sans serif. Exact clock text is sample content, not an identity constraint.

### Panel profile, material, and light

- **Observed:** The panel is a very softly rounded near-square, with an apparent corner radius about .08–.09 of its width. The perimeter is perceptible but quiet: a light upper rim and soft beige/gray shadow beneath and alongside it.
- **Observed:** The panel surface is cream/ivory, with gentle, broad tonal variation rather than a flat white field. The outside sheet background is slightly darker beige, allowing the panel to lift from it without a hard dark border.
- **Observed:** Light feels broad and diffuse. No internal object has a conspicuous sharp cast shadow, glossy highlight, or hard bevel. The branch has pictorial shading but is not a raised plastic decoration.
- **Test:** The perimeter should remain readable when ornament is removed. Inspect the corner transitions and bottom edge at full size: a sharp inset line, gray outline, or strong drop-shadow halo should not become a dominant shape.
- **Inference / uncertainty:** A paper/plaster-like surface is a useful material interpretation; the image does not prove literal paper grain. Preserve broad softness and tonal richness without making texture conspicuous.

### Background wash and its edge relationships

- **Observed:** A very pale warm beige/peach wash extends diagonally from the lower-left area toward the upper-right interior. Its upper-right lobe is broad and rounded, approaching x=.91 and y=.06–.38; its lower-left lobe approaches x=.04 and y=.88. Its strongest visible quality is a large, asymmetrical, soft organic silhouette behind the type.
- **Observed:** The wash is close in value to the panel and permits the cream ground to remain apparent. It has a discernible contour in places but no outline or dark shadow. It is much lighter than both rust time and olive text.
- **Observed:** The wash is largely inset from the left, top, and right panel boundaries. A smaller pale warm area is visible near the lower-right branch base and appears to meet both the bottom and right perimeters and rises behind the branch. The large wash and this lower-right area do not read as equally strong competing objects.
- **Test:** Temporarily ignore the text: the field should show an organic diagonal mass with unequal lobes and generous open cream space. It should not resolve into one centered perfect circle or a high-opacity flat peach badge. With the text restored, its contrast must stay subordinate.
- **Uncertainty after re-examination:** The low contrast makes it ambiguous whether the major shape is one continuous bean-like form or overlapping translucent lobes. Either construction may reproduce the observed silhouette and quiet transitions. Do not enforce an invented lobe count or exact internal overlap seam.

### Branch silhouette, attachment, and leaf character

- **Observed:** A single olive botanical sprig occupies the lower-right quadrant. It enters the panel close to the bottom-right edge, around (.97,.99), and spreads upward and left. Its total visible bounds are roughly x=.54–.98, y=.50–.99.
- **Observed:** There are approximately five principal leaves with deliberately different orientations and proportions: a tall terminal leaf turning up/right; a narrow right-side leaf rising diagonally; a smaller middle leaf turning up/left; a broad left-pointing leaf; and a lower leaf turning down/left. The result is asymmetric, with open spaces between leaves, rather than a repeated fan or evenly paired fern.
- **Observed:** Leaves are pointed, curved, and somewhat asymmetric across their central veins. Width is not constant; several have a fuller belly on one side and a tapering turn near the tip. The leftward leaf is broad and swept; the upper leaf is longer and more vertical. Those differences produce a recognizable silhouette before veins are visible.
- **Observed:** The stems are fine olive curves that converge into a coherent sprig. Leaf bases meet stems; the main line proceeds from the bottom-right entry toward the upper terminal leaf, while side stems curve toward the left-facing leaves. Leaves are not disconnected floating shapes, nor are all petioles rigid lines radiating from one hub.
- **Observed:** Central veins are visible within the leaves, with fine subsidiary diagonal vein marks. They follow the long axis and turns of each blade. The marks are lower contrast than the silhouette and do not look like bright engraved lines.
- **Observed:** Leaf surfaces vary in olive value, with darker/lighter regions that suggest curvature. Edges remain fairly crisp compared with the background wash. Stem and vein weight is appreciably thinner than leaf width.
- **Test:** At thumbnail size, the branch must still read as varied directional leaves on one curved sprig. At full size, inspect all five bases and tips: the bases should physically attach, veins should remain inside the blades, and tips should preserve distinct turns. Repeating one leaf shape at several angles is insufficient if it erases observed proportion and curvature differences.
- **Inference / uncertainty:** Exact leaf species, precise count of subsidiary veins, and literal physical lighting are not established. Botanical plausibility and the observed varied silhouette matter more than species naming or matching every small vein.

### Color, surface richness, layering, and figure-ground

- **Observed:** The palette has three principal roles: light ivory ground, pale warm wash, and darker olive type/branch, with rust time providing the strongest chromatic accent. Rust is muted brown-orange, not saturated red. Olive is muted yellow-green/brown-green, not cool emerald.
- **Observed:** Richness comes from broad ground variation, a faint wash, tonal variation within foliage, and fine vein detail. These details occur at different scales. None reads as uniform digital noise spread over every object.
- **Observed:** Layer order reads as panel surface → pale wash → date/time/AM and botanical sprig. Type stays legible over the wash. The branch visually belongs inside the panel and approaches its rounded bottom-right boundary.
- **Observed:** The tall leaf begins at approximately the level of the time's lower half, but remains to the right of the last digit. There is a narrow yet distinct cream gap, roughly .06–.08 panel width near the low numeral rows, with clearance depending on row, between the `4` and the nearest upper foliage. The branch spreads left below the numeral line while leaving `AM` clear. There is no foliage laid across the digits or date.
- **Observed:** The left-aligned text column and lower-right branch balance through unequal masses. The branch does not become a second central focal point; much of the upper/right region remains quiet.
- **Test:** Evaluate in grayscale as well as color: the time and secondary text must detach clearly from the pale wash, while the wash remains barely above the ground. The branch should have internal variation without becoming darker/heavier than the time as a whole. Check the time-to-leaf gap and AM-to-leaf clearance directly.

## Alternative readings considered and inventory lock

I re-examined the approved target after the first inventory pass. Plausible alternatives include a single lobed wash versus translucent overlapping ovals, a paper-like versus plaster-like panel, and different sturdy serif families for the time. None changes the visible requirements: asymmetric low-contrast wash; soft ivory panel; broad, characterful rust numerals; tracked olive date; larger separate olive AM; and a coherent lower-right sprig with varied curved leaves and subtle internal detail.

These normalized positions describe the square reference, not compulsory coordinates for every aspect ratio. In constrained compositions, date/time/branch may be repositioned or recomposed, and secondary wash ornament may be omitted. The defensible identity tests are hierarchy, glyph character, organic silhouette, attachment/leaf variety, palette, material softness, and clear figure-ground separation.

This is a reference inventory only. It contains no implementation comparison, grade, or approval conclusion. Await independent validation and implementation captures before critique.
