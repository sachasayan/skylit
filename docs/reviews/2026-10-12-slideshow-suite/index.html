Playback and interaction

A minimal player

Previous, Play/Pause, Zoom/Fit, Next and Close retain generous targets. Controls fade after four seconds. Refresh leaves the displayed scene, shuffled queue and remaining viewing time intact.

Five presentation styles

Falling prints keeps a restrained stack. Collage replaces one region while the other two stay in place. Gentle pan & zoom adds a slow, small drift; Still gallery removes animation. Reduced motion also freezes clips to their posters.

Portraits, whole pictures and surrounding space

Automatic composition pairs distinct portrait pictures. Fit avoids cropping by default. Fill, rotation, manual crop positions and local face-position suggestions are available for deliberate framing. Choose a theme mat, black or a soft picture-derived background.

A library you can curate

The gallery remains simple

The main screen keeps the library, Slideshow, Check now and reversible exclusion controls. Settings contains the presentation tools and photo source selector.

Keepers and collections

Protect important pictures with Keepers. Search, multi-select, favourite, exclude/restore and group photos into named collections. Arrange a presentation by grip or up/down controls, and save its options as a preset.

Framing and photo details

Titles and descriptions stay in Settings and accessibility metadata. The crop preview stays visible while editing. Capture dates are optional: new imports can retain a normalized date, or the owner can enter one. GPS and raw EXIF are discarded.

Media, schedules and phone curation

Local media and export

Import owner-selected pictures, muted H.264 MP4 clips and bounded GIFs through Android’s picker. An optional local AAC/M4A soundtrack follows pause/close. A titled presentation can be recorded locally to WebM in Downloads, with cancellation that leaves no partial file.

Automatic hours and brightness

Automatic photos follow the chosen city’s clock. Manual browsing remains available. Optional brightness changes Skylit’s window and restores system brightness when disabled; quiet hours stay independent.

Temporary phone curation

Start a 15-minute encrypted session in Settings. Compare the certificate fingerprint with the frame and use its one-time code. The phone can curate pictures, edit details, create collections, change basic slideshow settings and upload JPEG or muted H.264 MP4. It has no mail credentials, calendar feeds or debugging access. Stop, privacy pause, erase or account change revokes access.

Deliberate scope

  • Google shared albums keep Google’s player; Skylit’s curation, export and offline media tools apply to pictures stored on this frame.
  • Video is H.264 MP4, up to 30 seconds / 32 MiB. GIF conversion and AAC/M4A soundtracks are bounded; unsupported codecs report failure.
  • Export supports up to 12 still photos / 120 seconds. It records in real time and does not embed clips or reproduce every live motion effect. Exported Downloads files must be deleted separately from the private cache.
  • Phone management requires the same local Wi-Fi and a browser that permits certificate inspection/acceptance. It is off by default, expires and does not restart automatically.
  • Brightness affects the app window. There is no system sleep, power-on or unattended background-alert promise.
  • Local face suggestions and perceptual similarity are suggestions for review; there is no identity recognition or automatic deletion.
  • Short acceptance tests do not establish weeks of unattended playback or mail reliability.
