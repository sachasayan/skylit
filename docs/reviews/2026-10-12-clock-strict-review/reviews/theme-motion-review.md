## Theme findings

- Typography, display scale, label hierarchy, composition, and illustration geometry remain coherent in every palette/mode crop. No label or time loses usable visual contrast.
- The palette adaptations retain muted domestic surfaces and controlled lighting. Linen carries the literal ivory/olive/copper warmth; the other established palettes retain the same calm material and illustration language through their own hues. This is acceptance of the rendered palette adaptations, not an exemption for arbitrary color substitutions.
- Analog's dial remains readable in dark modes; the darker shallow rim is understated but still defines the disc. Hands and cardinal marks remain visible. Plant and pot retain enough tonal modeling to read as illustrated objects.
- Flip maintains large clear digits, restrained seams, hardware, and panel depth. The pale-panel/dark-digit reversal in several dark modes is coherent and preserves the physical mechanism. The vase's volume remains visible in both polarities.
- Botanical's organic ground remains subordinate to the time in dark modes, and its foliage retains clean contours and separation. No branch becomes a dense illegible mass.
- Landscape retains distinct smooth layers and paper-like surface variation. Its dark-mode sun/disc is intentionally subdued but still discernible; it is decorative, and clock readability remains strong. Terrain light/dark reversals produce a coherent stylized scene without a clipping or hierarchy issue.
- Contrast variants preserve defined curves and materials while providing clear information hierarchy. No halo, unreadable seam, or harsh outline creates a consequential finish problem.
