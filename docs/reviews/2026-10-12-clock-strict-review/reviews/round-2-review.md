## Basis for G1 acceptance

**Landscape:** poised, substantial olive serif time and restrained tracked labels sit in a warm paper-like sky. Ochre sun, olive foreground, and softly modeled terracotta hills form a coherent illustrated scene. The wide and strip scenes enter through clean rising curves at the base and preserve their deliberate split allocation. Tall's cropped large sun and quiet sky remain convincing. These are valid redraws rather than copies of the square target.

**Analog:** the luminous cream dial has a shallow rim, soft depth, warm brown restrained marks, copper hands, and an olive pivot. In the decorated variants, the vine has naturally tipped and curved leaves, varied turns, fine stems, and quiet tonal/vein detail; the trailing cluster reads as a plant. The warm terracotta pot and shallow wooden shelf establish the domestic material language without overwhelming the face. Dial-only minimum, tall, and strip remain coherent restrained adaptations.

**Flip:** large ivory sans-serif numerals carry the pair of matte olive panels. Rounded edges, subtle thickness and shadows, precise seams, fine surface variation, and restrained hardware establish a physical mechanism. The sprig uses tapering varied leaves and delicate branches. The pale vessel has a defined neck and shoulder, lit center/turning sides, and credible curved volume on the shelf. Its illustration is sufficiently finished at its current supporting size. Tall's stacked panels retain the same character.

**Botanical:** commanding warm terracotta serif time, smaller olive labels, and softly translucent organic background establish the editorial hierarchy. The branch has a curving stem, clean natural attachments, airy spacing, varied leaf widths/angles, smooth pointed curves, and subtle veins/tonal modeling. Full and near-square retain convincing botanical character without dense wedge-like clusters. The branch may be clipped at the edge in tall as a deliberate lower-right crop; it does not create an accidental fragment or conflict with text. Minimum and strip preserve the typographic identity while appropriately omitting foliage.

## Basis for G2 acceptance

- All shapes put the clock information first at a useful readable scale; labels remain distinct and secondary.
- The complete Analog strip dial/date group is balanced as one unit. The other narrow strips have coherent horizontal information groups.
- Flip tall stacking and dial-only Analog tall feel intentional rather than compressed.
- Landscape's side scenes and Botanical's asymmetric ground/branch retain stable visual weight. Their open space supports a calm hierarchy.
- Ornament size, cropping, and omission follow the available shape. No layout is rejected or excused solely by resemblance to the square reference.
- No visible time clipping, label collision, trapped label, or consequential near-tangency was observed in the native crops.

## Scope of acceptance

This is acceptance of static Linen light visual language and responsive composition in the seven supplied shapes, not a claim of runtime correctness or final theme/motion acceptance. The other palettes/modes and sampled motion remain to be reviewed under the same locked gates.
