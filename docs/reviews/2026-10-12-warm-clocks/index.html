Four warm clock widgets

Implemented the first four approved concepts: Analog, Landscape, Flip and Botanical. The existing four selections become these styles on upgrade. Choose one in Settings → Dashboard → Clock & date → Clock style, then Save dashboard.

Public screenshots use isolated synthetic data. Device screenshots and household settings stay private.

From concept to working widget

The reference supplies the visual direction. The implementation uses live time and date, responsive typography, themed surfaces and original local SVG/CSS artwork. Plant decoration disappears when space is tight.

Analog

A fine cream dial, lighter cardinal numerals, slimmer terracotta hands and an olive pivot. The date moves below the dial in narrow modules. A small local vector plant echoes the reference.

Landscape

Olive serif time, a separate day-period line and a soft terracotta landscape. The hills have a reserved region beneath the text; the sun stays circular as the card gets wider.

Flip

Horizontal olive flip tiles replace Stacked. A split seam, small hinges and soft shadows give depth. Only changed numbers animate; reduced motion and time jumps update immediately.

Botanical

Warm serif time replaces the technical monospace display. The translucent organic backdrop and plant echo the reference, with the date above and a quiet day-period label.

Designed for modular placement

TallAnalog centers its dial with the date beneath it. Flip stays horizontal; plants never replace the time.
Shallow stripFlip moves the date beside its tiles. Landscape and Botanical put the day period alongside the time.
Large or full moduleType and dial scale from measured module bounds with upper limits. Artwork stays subordinate to the time.
Theme and motionSix light/dark pairs remain supported. Flip runs only on changed numbers during visible normal ticks; first render, jumps, hidden documents and reduced motion update immediately.
