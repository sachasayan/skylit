All twelve inspected crops are the medium 496×300 composition. They preserve rounded hand construction, sparse marks, inner shaded/highlighted dial edge, and pot/wood/leaf layers. Theme hand/cap colors are palette adaptations; literal copper/olive color fidelity applies to Linen, not every theme.

In all dark crops the dial is shallower-looking than in Linen light, because the top shade is low contrast; the lower inner highlight still supplies the correct recess relationship. The plant/pot retain a brighter, warm illustrative light than the dark panel. This creates an accent, but its limited area and cohesive clay/wood/olive palette keep it integrated rather than resembling an unrelated sticker. It is visibly more prominent in dark mode, yet not a consequential hierarchy failure in these medium stills. This is a visual assessment, not a measured contrast-ratio or runtime acceptance.

## Remaining concrete finish differences and acceptance limits

Reference-faithful identity is accepted for the supplied static Linen views. The outer panel's convex lighting is softer, the face more uniform, copper bars somewhat lighter/more uniform, leaf veins sharper, and plant overlap follows the dial edge more closely. These are real finish/silhouette differences, but they do not contradict the inset construction or replace the illustrated object's defining parts. The panel's external cast shadow cannot be judged from tightly bounded native crops.

Theme presentation is accepted only for the twelve medium crops. Responsive acceptance is only for the seven supplied Linen dimensions.
