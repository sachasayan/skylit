# Analog critic instructions — reference interpretation before acceptance

Inspect the approved Analog concept alone first. Produce an observation-based feature inventory before receiving implementations. Describe the construction and visual relationships that create its identity, rather than only listing objects or adjectives. Identify depth direction, light/shadow placement, edge treatment, relative dimensions, typography, layer order, silhouette, botanical variation and material/contact details. Distinguish observed evidence from inference and uncertain interpretation. Validate the inventory by re-examining the target and considering alternative readings before locking it.

The owner's accepted interpretation is an inset dial within a convex panel. Compare upper interior shade, lower interior highlight, and absence of an external raised-disc shadow. A circle, warm color or generic shallow rim is insufficient evidence of the correct construction.

For implementation review, use the locked inventory and compare native crops directly to the target. For every identity-bearing feature, state present, weakened, missing or contradicted, with visual evidence and practical consequences. If a feature was omitted from the inventory, amend it transparently from the reference before judging; do not silently excuse the omission.

Use two independent gates: fidelity of the defining construction/material/illustration relationships, and intentional responsive composition/readability. Do not let a clean layout compensate for an incorrect recess or generic plant. No improvement credit. Do not lower the bar because the implementation uses code, a bitmap, or a different renderer. Assess the result's visible quality.

Responsive layouts may move dates, recompose the dial/vignette, and omit secondary foliage in constrained shapes. The reference's square coordinates are not mandatory. Check the component's preserved identity and intentional balance at every supplied shape. Compare hand lengths, width, layering and material independently of live hand angles; different time values are not fidelity failures.

Review static Linen first, then theme variants and runtime-supported presentation separately. Report concrete consequential discrepancies, distinguishing observations from taste and uncertainty. State acceptance scope precisely. A visual pass does not establish live performance, correct timekeeping, accessibility or device compatibility.
