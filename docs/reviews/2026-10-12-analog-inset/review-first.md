## Seven shapes, judged independently for composition

- **Minimum, 240×142:** Pass. Centered compact dial above a clear abbreviated date. Foliage omission is intentional and sensible. Numerals are small at native scale, but the four main positions and hands remain distinguishable. This does not establish accessibility at that size.
- **Wide, 496×194:** Pass. Dial and two-line date form a coherent horizontal group, and the tiny vignette occupies the dial's lower-right transition rather than a disconnected ornament. The foliage's fine details become less legible at this size, which is expected; the silhouette still reads as a plant.
- **Medium, 496×300:** Pass. Large centered dial, date below, and plant overlap at lower right form an intentional arrangement with clear spacing. No date collision or accidental shelf clipping appears.
- **Tall, 240×616:** Pass for this static shape. The clock/date group is centered within the narrow panel, with generous balanced space above/below. Omitting the plant protects readability. The tall layout is spare, but it is not visibly broken or accidentally stranded.
- **Strip, 1008×142:** Pass. A compact clock/date group is centered within the long strip. The dial and date are small but legible at native scale; the wide blank flanks are balanced. Foliage omission is appropriate. Scaling the clock to fill the entire strip is not required by the reference.
- **Full, 1008×616:** Pass composition. Dial/date scale appropriately, with a fully visible vignette and no hard collision. The date carries more visual weight than the reference's nonexistent date, but remains clearly subordinate to the clock group and is not a consequential layout defect. Fidelity differences remain visible most clearly here.
- **Near-square, 496×510:** Pass composition. The dial dominates, the date has clear space below, and the pot/shelf's right-edge crop reads intentional. Trailing leaves remain visible. This crop most directly exposes the higher shelf and shorter cascade relative to the reference, but those are fidelity concerns rather than a responsive layout failure.

## Consequential remaining work

The concave dial itself is visually accepted. To pass the complete reference gate, the priority discrepancies are the outer panel's shallow/flat edge treatment and the pot's missing separate lower base. Restore the reference's deeper shelf/cascade relationship and broader leaf mass so the vignette has its intended weight against the dial. The small shelf underside structure and copper material tuning are secondary but observable remaining differences.

No theme or runtime acceptance is granted from these Linen stills. The responsive acceptance covers only the seven supplied dimensions and static visual composition.

## Transparent correction after endpoint recheck
