Scan, compare, approve.

Phone curation now opens from a locally generated QR code. The phone and frame show the same random number; approval happens on the frame. No address or pairing number needs typing.

The connection flow

  1. Open Settings → Photos → Phone curation · local Wi-Fi and start a session.
  2. Scan the QR with a phone on the same Wi-Fi. Verify the local certificate against the fingerprint in Settings before accepting its warning.
  3. Compare the six-digit number on the two screens. Tap Numbers match · Allow on the frame, or Reject if they differ.
  4. Curate photos on the phone. Changes persist after the temporary connection ends.

Access stays under frame control

A scan creates a pending browser cookie with no photo, thumbnail, upload or settings access. Only the local frame can approve that exact pending request. The random number is a visual comparison, not an access credential.

  • QR invitation: single use, expires within five minutes.
  • Pending request: two minutes maximum, never beyond the invitation deadline.
  • Reject: revokes the pending cookie and creates a fresh QR while the invitation window remains open.
  • Session: fifteen minutes from starting it. Stop, privacy pause, erase and account changes revoke access.
  • The invitation stays in a URL fragment and is cleared from the phone’s address bar after opening. QR generation is offline.
