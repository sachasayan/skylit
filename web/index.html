Where do I find my calendar link?
  1. On your computer, open Google Calendar → Settings.
  2. Select a calendar under Settings for my calendars.
  3. Choose Integrate calendar and copy Secret address in iCal format.
  4. Paste it below. Repeat for each calendar you want here.

Keep this link private. Do not make your calendar public. If it is missing, use the calendar owner's account or check your organisation's settings.
