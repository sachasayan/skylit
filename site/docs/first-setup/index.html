Guides/Getting started

Set up your frame

Choose a city, connect your pictures and add the calendars you want to see.

Open the setup guide

A fresh installation opens Set up your frame. Existing installations keep their settings and are not interrupted by the guide after an update.

Reopen it at Settings → Help & maintenance → Open setup guide. You can leave setup and use the dashboard at any time.

Three things to choose

  1. City: open Settings → General → City & time, search for your city and select it. This sets weather and the timezone used by Skylit.
  2. Photos: choose a shared Google Photos album or pictures stored on the frame. For email delivery, use a dedicated Gmail account and enter its app password only on the frame.
  3. Calendar: open Calendar → Calendar options. Copy the secret iCal address on your computer and enter it directly on the frame. Add each calendar separately.

Never paste a secret calendar URL or app password into chat, a public issue, a screenshot or a setup record.

Check the result

Confirm the forecast shows the right city, a picture opens and advances in the slideshow, and an event change appears after a calendar refresh. A saved credential or an opened website alone does not prove the connection works.

Skylit has no hosted account or subscription. Google Home and YouTube use their own websites and sessions; sign in on the frame if needed.
