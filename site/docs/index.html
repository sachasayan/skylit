Getting started

  • Install Skylit

    Check your frame, install the signed app and keep a way back to its original launcher.

  • Set up your frame

    Choose a city, connect your pictures and add the calendars you want to see.

Make it yours

  • Arrange your dashboard

    Move, resize and style the widgets that make your frame useful at a glance.

  • Appearance, weather & Momo

    Set the colours, clock, city and companion for your room.

Photos

  • Get pictures onto the frame

    Choose a shared album or receive pictures through a dedicated Gmail inbox.

  • Photo library & slideshows

    Browse, keep, hide and present the pictures stored on your frame.

  • Manage photos from your phone

    Pair a nearby phone for a temporary, encrypted photo-management session.

Calendar

  • Connect your calendars

    Show read-only Google calendars through private iCal feeds, without a developer project.

Frame care

  • Update Skylit

    Download and install verified releases while keeping your frame’s settings.

  • Your data & privacy

    Understand what stays on your frame and how to remove or revoke access.

  • When something isn’t working

    Check connectivity, cached content and recovery options before changing your setup.
