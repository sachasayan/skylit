Questions

What hardware does Skylit run on?

Skylit is an Android app for wall-mounted photo frames with a landscape 16:9 or 16:10 screen. It is developed and verified on an Android 12 frame with WebView 95.

Do I need a subscription, cloud account or Google API key?

No. Skylit is free and has no hosted backend, so there is no Skylit account or subscription. It needs no Google API key, developer project or Google Play services.

How do photos get onto the frame?

Use a shared Google Photos album, or email pictures to a dedicated Gmail account whose app password you enter on the frame. You can also curate pictures from your phone over local Wi-Fi after pairing with a QR code.

How does the calendar work?

Copy each Google Calendar's secret address in iCal format on your computer and enter it on the frame. Skylit shows up to six calendars read-only and refreshes them every 15 minutes. You keep adding events on your phone as usual.

Where is my data stored?

On the frame. Calendar addresses, mail credentials and cached pictures stay in the app's private Android storage.

What happens when the internet drops?

The dashboard is bundled with the app, so it starts offline. The last forecast, calendar events and saved pictures stay available until the connection returns.

How does Skylit update?

From version 2.16, Skylit checks published GitHub releases, verifies the signed APK and can download or install it during an idle window you choose. Automatic download and installation are opt-in.

Is Skylit affiliated with Skylight or Wes Bos?

No. Skylit is an independent project inspired by Wes Bos's photo-frame-dashboard. It is not an official Wes Bos or Skylight product.
